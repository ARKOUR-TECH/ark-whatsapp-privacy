Last updated: August 11, 2026

When you send a message through WhatsApp, we may receive information provided by WhatsApp, such as your phone number, name or profile information, message content, message identifiers, and information necessary to process your request.

We use the information received through WhatsApp to receive and respond to messages, provide requested services, operate and improve our automation, and maintain the security and reliability of the service.

WhatsApp and Meta may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms. Our use of WhatsApp-related information is limited to operating the services described above.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers or technical systems that are necessary to operate the WhatsApp automation and provide the requested service.

5. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service, maintain security, comply with applicable obligations, or fulfill the purpose for which it was collected.

If you want your information deleted, please contact us using the contact information below. We will review and process valid deletion requests subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

We take reasonable measures to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

Our service is not intentionally directed toward children under the minimum age required by applicable law.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated date.

10. Contact

For questions or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us through the contact information associated with the application.
